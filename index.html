Borrow Safely

Protect your money and personal information.

01. Verify the Provider

Use official websites and verify the company's identity and regulatory status where applicable.

02. Understand the Cost

Check the interest rate, fees, repayment dates, penalties and the total amount you will repay.

03. Protect Your Details

Never share your PIN, password or one-time security code. Be cautious of requests for upfront loan-release fees.
